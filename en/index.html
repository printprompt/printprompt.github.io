Find who prints near you

On the map you see the makers in your area, their printers, materials and rates.

Open on Google Play

PrintPrompt is for Android and is in beta: for now only testers can download it.

How it works

  1. Use Explore to find makers near you: people with a 3D printer who print for others.
  2. Open a maker's profile to see their printers, materials and a price and time estimate.
  3. Send a request with your STL file: the maker makes you an offer, you confirm it and follow the print in the chat.

Describe it, AI models it

In the Create tab, describe an object in words: the AI assistant creates the 3D model and you adjust its measurements.

For makers

Become a maker: get requests from people nearby and set your own prices and availability.
