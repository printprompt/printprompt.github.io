Privacy policy

This policy explains what data the PrintPrompt app collects and how it uses it. It is the same one you find in the app (Profile → Info, privacy and terms), with a few more details.

Who is responsible

The data controller is [NOME E COGNOME]. For any question about your data, write to [EMAIL DI CONTATTO].

What data we collect

  • account: email and password (the password is encrypted and we can't see it);
  • trying without an account: if you use the app without signing up we create an anonymous access, with no name or email, and delete it after 30 days of inactivity;
  • maker profile, if you create one: display name, city, description, delivery options;
  • maker location: the exact one stays private; others only see the area, rounded to about 1 km;
  • printers and photos you upload, with the rates you set (a photo is required for every printer);
  • your current location, only if you tap "Near me": it centres the map and finds the makers around you; we don't store it;
  • the STL files you open: we read them on the phone for sizes and quotes. If you send a request, the file goes to a private space and only the maker you chose can see it;
  • reviews: they're public and show your shortened name (e.g. "Mario R.") and the printed material;
  • makers' profile photos and work galleries: they're public within the app;
  • requests and messages: you and the maker can see them. The administrator reads them only to check a report or a safety issue. The maker sees your shipping address only after you accept their offer. Your email is never shown to other users;
  • reports: if you report something, we store the reason, the text you write and a copy of the reported content. The person you report can't see who you are; the administrator can (name and email), to handle the report;
  • notices: when the administrator decides on a report, you get a notice in the app (both whoever reported and whoever was reported);
  • technical data on errors: if the app closes because of an error, on the next start it sends us the type of error, where in the code it happened, the phone model and the Android and app versions, linked to your account. We use them only to fix problems;
  • AI assistant: what you write in the Create tab and the model's code are sent to Anthropic (Claude), the service that generates the models. Don't write personal data in your descriptions. We save your models in your account and a log of how many requests you make, for the daily limits.
  • phone notifications: to send you notifications we store a code for your phone, issued by Firebase (Google), linked to your account. We remove it when you sign out; notifications can be turned off in the phone settings.

Why and on what basis

Why: to run the service (accounts, maker search, estimates) and keep it safe. We don't sell data and don't use it for advertising.

We use the data to provide the service you ask for when you create the account (performance of a contract, Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). Technical error data and copies of reported content keep the app working and safe (legitimate interest, Art. 6(1)(f)).

Where the data is and who processes it for us

  • Supabase: database, sign-in and files (photos and STL).
  • Anthropic: generates the AI assistant's models. It only receives what you write in the Create tab and the model's code, not your name or email.
  • OpenFreeMap: provides the maps; like any website, it receives the phone's IP address when it downloads the area you are viewing.
  • Google Firebase: delivers notifications to the phone (it receives the phone's code and the text of the notification). Through Crashlytics and Performance it also receives, if the app crashes or is slow, the type of error, the phone model, the Android and app versions and loading times, without your name or email.
  • GitHub: hosts this website.

Some of these providers may process data outside the European Union (for example in the United States), with the safeguards required by the GDPR.

How long we keep it

We keep the data as long as you have the account. When you delete it, we erase your data straight away, with the exceptions explained on the Deleting your account and data page.

Your rights

Your rights: you can view and correct your data from your Profile, and delete everything with "Delete account". You can also ask for a copy of your data or complain to the Italian Data Protection Authority (Garante).

To ask for a copy of your data, correct it, erase it or object to processing, write to [EMAIL DI CONTATTO]: we reply within one month. You can also complain to the data protection authority in your country.

This website

The website uses no cookies, analytics or external services. GitHub, which hosts it, logs visitors' IP addresses for security reasons.
