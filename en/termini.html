Terms of use

  • PrintPrompt connects people who want to print an object with makers in their area. The service is in beta.
  • You must be at least 18 to use PrintPrompt.
  • Prices and times shown are estimates: the maker proposes the final price and the customer accepts it before printing.
  • For now, payment is agreed directly between customer and maker, outside the app.
  • Each maker is responsible for their prints and for what they state on their profile.
  • Every printer must have a real photo, taken by whoever registers it. By uploading it you let us use it, with your name, as the photo of that model in the catalogue. If you delete your account you can ask us to remove it.
  • Photos must not contain recognisable people, personal data or other people's images without permission.
  • Reviews can only be left after a delivery and must describe a real experience. We may hide offensive, false or off-topic ones.
  • The "Verified maker" badge is given by PrintPrompt after checking the maker's profile and printers; it can be removed if the information is no longer correct.
  • The app must not be used for illegal or dangerous objects (for example weapons or weapon parts) or objects that infringe others' rights.
  • Models created with the AI assistant may contain errors: check sizes and printability before printing. Each user has a limited number of creations per day; adjusting sizes with the sliders is always free.
  • You can report profiles, photos, reviews and requests that break these rules: tap ⋮ or "Report". False or spiteful reports are not allowed.
  • We review every report and may remove photos, hide reviews or suspend accounts. Everyone involved gets a notice in the app with the reason for the decision.
