Deleting your account and data

You can delete your PrintPrompt account and the related data at any time: from the app, or by writing to us if you no longer have the app.

From the app (immediately)

  1. Open PrintPrompt and go to the “Profile” tab.
  2. Tap “Delete account”, at the bottom of the page.
  3. Type DELETE to confirm and tap “Delete forever”.
  4. The account and data are deleted immediately and permanently.

Without the app (by email)

Write to [EMAIL DI CONTATTO] from the address you signed up with, with the subject "Account deletion". We reply to confirm and delete the account within 30 days. To protect you, we only delete the account that matches the address you write from.

What is deleted

  • the account: email and password;
  • the maker profile, location, printers, profile photo, gallery and printer photos;
  • print requests sent and received, with their STL files, messages and reviews;
  • favourites, notices and the models created with the AI assistant.

What remains, no longer linked to your account

  • reports already sent or received, with the copy of the reported content: they are needed to handle abuse;
  • technical data about app errors and the count of AI assistant requests (needed for costs);
  • printer photos chosen for the model catalogue, with the name you had: if you want them removed, write to us.
